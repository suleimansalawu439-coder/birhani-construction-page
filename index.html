About Us

Building Exceptional Environments, Delivering Lasting Value

Welcome to Birhani Construction Limited. We began several years ago as a building contracting and landed property consultancy and solicitation business. Today, we continue to build on our experience, professionalism and passion for excellence to provide quality services to individuals, families, businesses, investors and organizations.

Birhani Construction Ltd is a comprehensive real estate and construction company focused on contributing to the transformation of the Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Abuja — and Nigeria at large — through innovative property development and infrastructural construction.

At Birhani Construction Limited, we believe that every project represents an opportunity to create something exceptional. We therefore approach every assignment with professionalism, attention to detail, transparency and a strong commitment to quality.

Our Mission

To provide our customers with excellence in real estate and building construction through technology-driven, state-of-the-art modern designs, professional services and quality project delivery.
